- **Earshot tells you when something goes wrong.** The menu lists each problem and, where there is one, a way to fix it: a microphone that isn't allowed, a speech engine that didn't start or stopped, a lost connection. A session that can't go on ends and keeps what was transcribed so far, instead of looking like it's still listening, and the ear in the menu bar shows a mark until you look.
- **Translation asks before it downloads.** When a language needs Apple's download, the menu says so, and **Download…** opens the prompt. Languages Apple can't translate are named too.
- **The Earshot window shows in the Dock and ⌘Tab** while it's open, like any other window.
- **One copy at a time.** Opening Earshot while it's already running points you to the menu bar instead.
- **Quitting while listening no longer freezes Earshot.** It finishes the session, saves the transcript, and then quits.
- **The window is about your transcripts.** The live session sits on top of your saved transcripts, grouped by day. Each line is a card with the speaker's colour, in bigger text you can change with ⌘+ and ⌘−, and its translation under it; a switch shows the original, the translation, or both. Words still being spoken wait in a bar at the bottom instead of changing the last line, and the window follows new lines only while you're at the bottom. Copy, Show in Finder, Summarize, and Name Speakers are in the toolbar, and a summary that fails says so over the transcript, with **Try Again**. Words, Models, and About moved into a standard Settings window (⌘,).
