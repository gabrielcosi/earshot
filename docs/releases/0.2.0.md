- **Earshot tells you when something goes wrong.** The menu lists each problem and, where there is one, a way to fix it: a microphone that isn't allowed, a speech engine that didn't start or stopped, a lost connection. A session that can't go on ends and keeps what was transcribed so far, instead of looking like it's still listening, and the ear in the menu bar shows a mark until you look.
- **Translation asks before it downloads.** When a language needs Apple's download, the menu says so, and **Download…** opens the prompt. Languages Apple can't translate are named too.
- **The Earshot window shows in the Dock and ⌘Tab** while it's open, like any other window.
- **One copy at a time.** Opening Earshot while it's already running points you to the menu bar instead.
- **Quitting while listening no longer freezes Earshot.** It finishes the session, saves the transcript, and then quits.
- **The window is about your transcripts.** The live session sits on top of your saved transcripts, grouped by day. Each line is a card with the speaker's colour, in bigger text you can change with ⌘+ and ⌘−, and its translation under it; a switch shows the original, the translation, or both. Words still being spoken wait in a bar at the bottom instead of changing the last line, and the window follows new lines only while you're at the bottom. Copy, Show in Finder, Summarize, and Name Speakers are in the toolbar, and a summary that fails says so over the transcript, with **Try Again**. Words, Models, and About moved into a standard Settings window (⌘,).
- **Play a transcript's kept audio.** A transcript saved with **Keep audio** opens with a player above it: play or pause with Space, click the waveform to jump anywhere, or a line's play button to listen from that line. The line playing is highlighted. Both sides play in both ears, and so do the clips in **Name Speakers**.
- **Choose how good kept audio sounds.** **Audio Quality** in **Settings > General** has Low, Medium, and High: about 23, 35, and 45 MB an hour. Medium and High keep more of the sound's detail than the 16 kHz the speech engine hears. A change applies from the next session. With **Cancel speaker echo** on, your microphone is kept as the engine hears it.
- **Problems in the menu can be dismissed.** Each one has a close button; it comes back if it happens again.
- **Transcripts are kept in Earshot's own library.** Each one is saved after every finished line, and a Markdown copy is written to your transcripts folder when the session ends and again after you name the speakers or add a summary. If you change or delete that copy outside Earshot, Earshot never writes over it or brings it back: the transcript says the file is out of date, and **Export…** writes a new one. Two sessions started in the same minute get two files. A session cut short by a crash is finished and written out the next time Earshot opens.
- **Your 0.1 transcripts come along.** The first time Earshot 0.2 opens, it imports the transcripts in your transcripts folder, with their titles, names, translations, summaries, and kept audio, and shows its progress at the bottom of the sidebar. The files are left exactly as they are. A file that matches what Earshot writes is kept up to date; any other is left alone. Files Earshot can't read as transcripts are listed in **Settings > General**.
- **Kept audio stays with Earshot.** It is no longer saved next to the Markdown file. **Show Audio in Finder** and **Export Audio…** are under **Show in Finder** in the toolbar. **Export Audio…** mixes both sides into one channel, so the file plays in both ears in any player.
- **Suggested speaker names quote only the sentence that says the name.** A speaker whose name is never said gets no suggestion, and the sheet says no name was found.
