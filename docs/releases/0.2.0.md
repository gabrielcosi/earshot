- **A redesigned window**: transcripts grouped by day, bigger text (⌘+ and ⌘−), and Settings in its own window (⌘,).
- **Start listening from the window**: top of the sidebar, or ⌘N; ⌘. stops.
- **See it listening**: the running time, a level meter per source, and Stop, right under the live transcript.
- **Captions over any app**, even a full-screen call: turn on **Captions Overlay** in the menu.
- **The live transcript follows new lines** until you scroll up; **Jump to Latest** brings you back.
- **Name speakers in a side panel**, with talk time, lines to play, suggested names, and undo.
- **Play kept audio** with a waveform, Space, or from any line.
- **Choose how good kept audio sounds**: Low, Medium, or High.
- **Transcripts live in Earshot's library**, and an exported Markdown file you edited is never overwritten.
- **Your 0.1 transcripts come along**; the original files stay untouched.
- **Kept audio stays in Earshot**; export it from **Show in Finder**.
- **Earshot tells you when something goes wrong**, in the menu, with a way to fix it, and in the window when listening can't start.
- **Translation asks before it downloads** a language.
- **The window shows in the Dock and ⌘Tab.**
- **Quitting while listening no longer freezes Earshot.**
