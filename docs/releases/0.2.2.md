- **Translation sticks to your Spoken languages**: short words such as "Ja." are no longer read as another language and reported as needing a download.
- **Earshot tells you when it hears a language you didn't choose** under **Spoken**, with two or more chosen, shows those lines as they were said, and translates them once you add it in one click.
- **Close notices in the captions overlay** with ✕, as in the menu.
- **The captions overlay closes as soon as you stop listening**, while Earshot finishes the transcript.
- **Stopping a long session is much faster**: telling the speakers apart in a 40-minute meeting takes seconds rather than over a minute, and the audio is saved meanwhile.
- **Speakers are sorted out after sessions over about half an hour too**; Earshot used to give up waiting and keep the live speakers. If it fails, the menu says so.
- **Words are no longer dropped when Earshot rechecks who said what** at the end of a session.
