Hello, Earshot! 👂

- **Transcribe anything your Mac plays**, live: calls, meetings, videos, podcasts. You're "Me", and up to eight other voices get told apart.
- **Name the speakers** when you stop, with a few of their lines to read and play.
- **Dozens of languages, even mixed in one call**, with live translation into yours.
- **Pick the apps to listen to**: your call, not the music.
- **Take calls on speakers** with echo cancellation.
- **Summaries** with decisions and action items.
- **All on your Mac**, saved as plain Markdown.

Apple silicon, macOS 26.4 or later.
